**Subject: Non-charity registration — enquiry from a fundraising
platform launching shortly**

Hello,

I run favpoll (favpoll.com), a UK platform launching shortly. We are a
company, not a charity. Someone marking an occasion (e.g. a memorial
service) sets up a page and chooses a registered charity. Guests pledge
small amounts. We deduct nothing: 100% of a pledge goes to the charity.
Card processing is charged separately to the donor at checkout, shown as
its own line, and we retain none of it. Our only income is an optional
contribution a guest may add on top of their pledge. We intend to
collect as agent for the charity, with a written agreement in place, and
funds reach the charity by bank transfer within 10 working days of the
closing date.

I put four questions to your Code Advice Service in late August, and
Oliver kindly replied by phone on 1 September. On registration, he said
non-charity registration may be open to us and suggested I contact your
team for the specifics.

1. Can a company in our position register with the Fundraising
   Regulator?

2. What does the application involve, and how long does it typically
   take? We would like registration in place before launch.

3. How is the registration fee determined for a platform with no
   fundraising history yet?

4. We are taking legal advice on whether Part II of the Charities Act
   1992 applies to us as a professional fundraiser. Can registration
   proceed in parallel with that, or does the application depend on the
   answer?

Once registered, we would display the badge at the point of donation. I
am happy to talk it through on a call, or to send a link to a working
demonstration if that is easier than a description.

Best wishes,

Joseph Moffatt
Josmo Services Limited, trading as favpoll
