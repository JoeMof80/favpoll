**Subject: Non-charity registration — enquiry from a fundraising
platform launching shortly**

Dear registration team,

I run favpoll (favpoll.com), a UK platform launching shortly. We are a
company, not a charity, and we would like to register with the
Fundraising Regulator — we expect the charities we work with to check.

What favpoll does:

- Someone marking an occasion (e.g. a memorial service) sets up a page
  and chooses a registered charity. Guests pledge small amounts.
- We deduct nothing: 100% of a pledge goes to the charity.
- Card processing is charged separately to the donor at checkout, shown
  as its own line, and we retain none of it.
- Our only income is an optional contribution a guest may add on top of
  their pledge.
- We intend to collect as agent for the charity, under a written
  agreement, and to pass Gift Aid declarations to the charity to claim
  rather than claiming anything ourselves.
- Funds reach the charity by bank transfer within 10 working days of
  the closing date.

I put four questions to your Code Advice Service in late August, and
Oliver kindly replied by phone on 1 September. On registration, he said
non-charity registration may be open to us and suggested I contact your
team for the specifics. Having read the non-charity registration pages,
I have four questions:

1. Your eligibility criteria ask for "a record of operating in a
   fundraising capacity for at least 12 months". We are pre-launch, so
   we do not yet have that record. Can a platform in our position
   register now — and if not, when does the 12 months start to run:
   from incorporation, from launch, or from our first donation?

2. Registration fees are scaled by fundraising turnover, and ours to
   date is nil. Which fee band would apply to a newly launched
   platform?

3. Beyond the published steps (familiarity with the Code, a published
   complaints policy, and the application form), is there anything else
   you would expect a non-charity platform to have in place before
   applying?

4. We are taking legal advice on whether Part II of the Charities Act
   1992 applies to us as a professional fundraiser. Can registration
   proceed in parallel with that, or does the application depend on the
   answer?

Once registered, we would display the badge at the point of donation. I
am happy to talk it through on a call, or to send a link to a working
demonstration if that is easier than a description.

Best wishes,

Joseph Moffatt
Josmo Services Limited, trading as favpoll
