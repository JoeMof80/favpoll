Subject: Fixed-fee opinion — Charities Act 1992 Part II status of a donations platform

Dear [name],

I run favpoll (favpoll.com), a UK platform — a trading name of Josmo
Services Limited — through which guests at occasions pledge money to

We need a written opinion on whether we are a "professional

Two things would help us choose advisers: whether this is work you
cover on a fixed fee, and a likely range; and whether you typically
act for fundraising platforms and commercial participators, or
primarily for charities — and whether your charity client base would
raise conflicts in acting for us on agreements with individual
charities.

Happy to send the full instructions on.
