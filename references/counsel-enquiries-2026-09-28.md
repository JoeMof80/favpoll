## 1. To Clare Lawrence (Wrigleys)

**Subject: favpoll — fixed-fee opinion, Part II Charities Act 1992**

Dear Clare,

I run favpoll (favpoll.com), a recently launched UK platform where guests pledge money to registered charities at life events.

We need a written opinion on whether we are a "professional fundraiser" and/or "commercial participator" under Part II of the Charities Act 1992 and the 1994 Regulations, what any such status requires of our charity agreements and donor solicitation statements, and how our operating sequences fare under the Act. The Fundraising Regulator has confirmed the classification needs legal advice. We have a two-page instruction letter ready setting out the model and the precise questions, and a draft charity agreement that would later need reviewing.

Could you let me know if this is work you would take on for a fixed fee and what that is likely to be? It would also help to know whether you act for platforms like ours as well as for charities, and, given your charity client base, whether conflicts could arise in acting for a platform on its agreements with individual charities.

Separately, and second to the opinion, we will need our terms of service and privacy policy reviewed; an interim privacy policy is live and the terms are a draft. If that is work your team would also take on, a separate figure for it would be welcome.

Best wishes,

## Fallback: a barrister by Direct Access

If all four firms quote slowly or badly, a chancery junior with charity
regulation experience can give a written opinion on the bounded Part II
question at a fixed fee, often within a fortnight — the clerks at
Radcliffe Chambers, 5 Stone Buildings or Serle Court place it. The
opinion decides the statutory point and the solicitation statement
wording; terms, privacy and the agreement stay solicitor work.

Dear Clerks,

I run favpoll (favpoll.com), a recently launched UK platform where guests pledge money to registered charities at life events, and I am looking for a written opinion under the Direct Access scheme.

The question is bounded: whether favpoll is a "professional fundraiser" and/or "commercial participator" under Part II of the Charities Act 1992 and the Charitable Institutions (Fund-Raising) Regulations 1994; what any such status requires of our agreements with charities and of the solicitation statements made to donors; and how our operating sequences fare under the Act. The Fundraising Regulator has confirmed the classification turns on legal advice. A two-page instruction letter setting out the model and the precise questions is ready, with a draft charity agreement for context.

Would a member of chambers with charity regulation experience take this on Direct Access for a fixed fee, and what would that fee and timescale be? I am a private limited company, not a charity, and would be the client directly.

Best regards,

Joseph Moffatt
Josmo Services Limited, trading as favpoll
