**Subject: Regulatory advice — is a nil-deduction fundraising platform
a "professional fundraiser" under Part II of the Charities Act 1992?**

Hello,

I run favpoll (favpoll.com), a UK tech startup launching shortly, and I
would like to apply for advice from the clinic.

**Eligibility:** favpoll is a trading name of Josmo Services Limited, a
UK private limited company. We are pre-launch, with annual turnover
under £100,000.

**What favpoll does:**

- Someone marking an occasion (e.g. a memorial service) sets up a page
  and chooses a registered charity. Guests pledge small amounts.
- We deduct nothing: 100% of a pledge goes to the charity.
- Card processing is charged separately to the donor at checkout, shown
  as its own line, and we retain none of it.
- Our only income is an optional contribution a guest may add on top of
  their pledge.
- We intend to collect as agent for the charity, under a written
  agreement, and to pass Gift Aid declarations to the charity to claim
  rather than claiming anything ourselves.
- Funds reach the charity by bank transfer within 10 working days of
  the closing date.

**The legal question:** the Fundraising Regulator has told us (by phone,
1 September 2026) that whether we are a "professional fundraiser" or
"commercial participator" under Part II of the Charities Act 1992 turns
on legal advice they cannot give. Specifically, we would like advice
on:

1. Whether favpoll, as described, is a professional fundraiser and/or a
   commercial participator under Part II of the Charities Act 1992 and
   the associated 1994 Regulations, given that our deduction from
   donations is nil and our only income is an optional contribution the
   donor chooses to add.

2. If either status applies: what our written agreement with each
   charity must contain to satisfy the statutory requirements (we have
   a working draft), and what solicitation statement we must make to
   donors — in particular how to state, accurately, that the deduction
   from their pledge is nil.

3. If neither status applies: whether anything else in the 1992 Act or
   the Regulations constrains how we solicit, hold, or transmit the
   funds.

There is no dispute involved — this is regulatory analysis for a
pre-launch product. I can share our draft charity agreement, the exact
checkout copy, and a link to a working demonstration. I am happy to
attend in person or online, whichever suits the clinic.

Your website still shows the clinic as closed to new enquiries, with
reopening due in July/August — if the intake is not yet open, I would be
grateful to be queued for when it is, or to hear when I should apply.
