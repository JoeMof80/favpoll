### What favpoll collects

**If you create a favpoll (an organiser):**

- Your account: name and email address, held by our sign-in provider
  (Clerk). If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email
  and profile photo with us.
- What you write and upload: the favpoll's about text, the reveal,
  and any photo. If your favpoll is about another living person,
  you are responsible for having their agreement to share their
  photo and details.

**If you pledge (a guest):**

- Your email address, the name you choose to display, the favourite
  you pick and the amount you pledge.
- You can appear in the guest book as "Someone" instead of your
  name. The guest book shows names and favourites — never what any
  one person gave.

**Payments:**

- Payments are processed by Stripe. favpoll never sees or stores
  your card number. We keep a record of each payment (amount,
  reference) so every pledge can be accounted for and passed to the
  charity.

**Technical:**

- Like every website, our hosting providers record standard
  technical logs (IP address, browser type) to run and secure the
  service.
- Cookies: favpoll uses essential cookies only — the ones that keep
  you signed in. There is no advertising tracking.

### What favpoll does with it

- Runs your favpoll: showing the poll, the standings and the guest
  book, and delivering the reveal after a pledge.
- Passes what is raised to the chosen registered charity, in full.
- Sends service emails (for example, confirming a pledge). favpoll
  does not send marketing email.
- Adds your favourite — anonymised, with no name attached — to
  favpoll's all-time record of favourites.
