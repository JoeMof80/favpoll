### What favpoll is

favpoll lets an organiser create a poll of favourites for an
occasion or a cause. Guests pledge money to a registered charity,
pick their own favourite, and see where it stands among the others.

**Nothing is won.** favpoll is not a raffle, lottery, prize
competition or betting service. There are no prizes, no odds and no
draw. The standings only show where the giving went, and every
pledge reaches the charity regardless of which favourite leads.

### Pledges and money

- A pledge is a gift to the favpoll's chosen registered charity —
  never to the organiser, and never to favpoll.
- favpoll charges no platform fee: 100% of every pledge reaches the
  charity. Payments are processed by Stripe. favpoll is sustained by
  optional supporter contributions, which are always separate from
  your pledge.
- Pledges are gifts and are not refundable once made.

### Organisers

By creating a favpoll you confirm that:

- Anything you upload or write is yours to share. If your favpoll is
  about a living person, you have their agreement — including for
  their photo.
- Your favpoll is honest: the person, occasion and cause are real,
  and the reveal is written in good faith.
- You will not use favpoll for anything unlawful, hateful or
  deceptive. favpoll may unpublish or remove a favpoll that breaks
  these terms.

A favpoll runs for at most 90 days and closes automatically. A
closed favpoll cannot be edited.

### Guests

- One active guest pledge per email address per poll. Signed-in
  guests may pledge more than once.
- The guest book shows your chosen name and favourite — never your
  amount. You may appear as "Someone".
- The reveal is written by the organiser; favpoll is not responsible
  for its contents.
