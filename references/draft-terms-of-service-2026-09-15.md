### What favpoll is

favpoll lets an organiser create a poll of favourites for an
occasion or a cause. Guests pledge money to a registered charity,
pick their own favourite, and see where it stands among the others.

**Nothing is won.** favpoll is not a raffle, lottery, prize competition
or betting service. There are no prizes, no odds and no draw, and no
guest receives anything of value for pledging. Every pledge goes to the
charity regardless of which favourite leads.

On some favpolls the organiser has said in advance that the picks decide
something on the day — which songs get played, say. That is the
organiser's own arrangement with their guests. Nothing is won from
favpoll, and the outcome changes nothing about where the money goes.

### Pledges and money

- A pledge is a gift to the favpoll's chosen registered charity —
  never to the organiser, and never to favpoll.
- **favpoll charges no platform fee and takes nothing from your pledge.**
  Payments are processed by Stripe, and the cost of processing is met
  from the pledge at settlement, so a charity receives the pledge less
  its share of processing. favpoll is sustained by optional supporter
  contributions, which are always separate from your pledge.
  *(The earlier wording — "100% of every pledge reaches the charity" —
  was retired on 6 September 2026 when processing moved to settlement.
  It must not come back.)*
- A favpoll cannot accept any pledge until every charity on it has
  agreed to receive them. Until then the pledge button is withheld.

### Organisers

By creating a favpoll you confirm that:

- Anything you upload or write is yours to share. If your favpoll is
  about a living person, you have their agreement — including for
  their photo.
- Your favpoll is honest: the person, occasion and cause are real,
  and anything you write is written in good faith.
- You will not use favpoll for anything unlawful, hateful or
  deceptive. favpoll may unpublish or remove a favpoll that breaks
  these terms.

A favpoll runs for at most 90 days and closes automatically. A
closed favpoll cannot be edited.

### Guests

- One active guest pledge per email address per poll. Signed-in
  guests may pledge more than once.
- The guest book shows your chosen name and favourite — never your
  amount. You may appear as "Someone".
- The personal note, where there is one, is written by the organiser;
  favpoll is not responsible for its contents.
