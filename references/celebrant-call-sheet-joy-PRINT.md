The app grew partly out of that day. Before launch I want a
professional eye on whether it's **appropriate and ethical** — she's the
right person, not a sales target. *"Mind if I take notes?"*

**Remember:** listen 80/20 · ask about her *recent* services, not
"in general" · no demo until the end · on Q4 and Q7, watch her
reaction more than her words.

## If she asks — facts at your fingertips

- Guests **pledge to charity** and share a favourite; the person's own
  favourite is revealed after pledging.
- **No fee — 100% of every pledge reaches the charity** (favpoll runs
  on optional tips).
- **Registered UK charities only** — never funeral costs, never
  personal funds.
- Guests need **no account** — email only; payments by card, Apple Pay
  or Google Pay.
- A shared fund can cover guests who can't pay (children, etc.) —
  privately.
